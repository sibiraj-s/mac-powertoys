Apps no longer shows updates for an up-to-date Android Studio or Claude. Android Studio and JetBrains IDEs such as IntelliJ IDEA and PyCharm are now checked against the list of releases they update from, on the channel they're on, and show their full version, such as 2026.2.2 Patch 1. Claude is checked with its own update service. Both used to be estimated from Homebrew's list, which can be ahead of what an app offers. Apps that share an update list, such as several JetBrains IDEs, download it once per check.
