Swiping a Lanes row on the trackpad now steps it one window at a time, sliding smoothly like the mouse wheel, instead of dragging every window along with your fingers and snapping back. A short flick moves one window; keep swiping for more. The overview follows your fingers, and the coasting after you lift no longer moves the row. Switching tabs in an app with native tabs, such as Ghostty, no longer rearranges the row: the tab you switch to takes the place of the one before.
