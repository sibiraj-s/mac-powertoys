Cleaner can now clean temporary files in /tmp and your own temporary folder, which macOS no longer clears while your Mac stays on. By default, only what hasn't been used for 3 days goes; a folder with something newer in it stays, and only its older contents go. Turn on "Clean everything, however new" to remove everything not in use. Files in use, sockets and pipes running apps use to talk to each other, and other users' files are always left alone.
