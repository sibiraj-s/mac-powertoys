Apps: a new module for the apps in your Applications folders. Uninstall one with its containers, settings, caches, logs, shared containers and launch agents, each with a checkbox, sorted by name, size or when it was last opened; untick the app to remove only its data and start it afresh. Everything goes to the Trash. Check for updates looks up newer versions in the App Store, apps' own Sparkle update feeds and Homebrew, without installing anything
